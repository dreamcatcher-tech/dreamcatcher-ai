The difference

Machine speed, human judgment.

Dreamcatcher agents are not sold as pure automation. They are mediated systems: software does the work it is good at, while people inspect the failure modes, tighten the instructions, improve the tools, and step in when the agent needs help.

Behavior is maintained We watch for drift, brittle responses and missing context, then patch the agent instead of blaming the user.
Approvals stay explicit Payments, messages, account changes and other risky actions are designed to pass through approval boundaries.
Confidentiality is a product direction Secure enclaves and stronger secrets isolation are part of the roadmap. Current controls are presented honestly, not overstated.
