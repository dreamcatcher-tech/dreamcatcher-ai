<div class="dc-shell">
  <div class="dc-kicker">Naming decision</div>
  <div class="dc-grid-2">
    <div>
      <h2>Choose Ark.</h2>
      <p class="dc-bigline">Core is architecture. Ark is a thing.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="dc-card">
      <h3>Why Ark lands</h3>
      <p>It carries precious continuity. It is durable, portable, protected, and specific enough to talk about in ordinary language.</p>
      <div class="dc-pill-row"><span class="dc-pill">custody</span><span class="dc-pill">protection</span><span class="dc-pill">carriage</span><span class="dc-pill">continuity</span><span class="dc-pill">ownership</span></div>
    </div>
  </div>
</div>

---

<div class="dc-shell">
  <div class="dc-kicker">The problem with old names</div>
  <div class="dc-grid-3" style="margin-top: 20px;">
    <div class="dc-card"><h3>Core</h3><p>Technically useful, but generic. It sounds like an implementation detail.</p></div>
    <div class="dc-card"><h3>Container</h3><p>Correct but bland. It misses the preciousness and the machinery inside.</p></div>
    <div class="dc-card"><h3>Universe</h3><p>Captures “everythingness,” but loses bounded custody and can sound too vast.</p></div>
  </div>
  <p class="dc-bigline" style="margin-top: 30px; max-width: 1000px;">Ark says: this is the durable vessel the student can carry forward.</p>
</div>

---

<div class="dc-shell">
  <div class="dc-kicker">The Ark</div>
  <div class="dc-grid-2" style="grid-template-columns: 1.05fr .95fr;">
    <div>
      <h2>A protected enduring vessel.</h2>
      <p class="dc-bigline">The Ark is the student-owned place where knowledge, tools, memory, values, and agency accumulate.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="dc-card">
      <h3>How to say it</h3>
      <ul>
        <li>“Every student has an Ark.”</li>
        <li>“The Ark belongs to the student, not the vendor.”</li>
        <li>“The school can certify and contribute without owning the Ark.”</li>
        <li>“A student can leave with their Ark.”</li>
      </ul>
    </div>
  </div>
</div>

---

<div class="dc-shell">
  <div class="dc-kicker">The Steward</div>
  <div class="dc-grid-2">
    <div>
      <h2>The active intelligence inside.</h2>
      <p class="dc-bigline">The Steward tends the Ark. It works for the student without becoming the owner.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="dc-card">
      <h3>Why this name</h3>
      <p>“Agent” is generic. “Worker” is too mechanical. “Daemon” is technically rich but risky for parents. Steward has the right duty: active care on behalf of someone else.</p>
      <h3 style="margin-top: 22px;">Common language</h3>
      <p>“Your Steward can change models over time, but your Ark keeps continuity.”</p>
    </div>
  </div>
</div>

---

<div class="dc-shell">
  <div class="dc-kicker">Models are engines, not identity</div>
  <div class="dc-grid-2">
    <div class="dc-card">
      <h3>What changes</h3>
      <p>AI models, tools, retrieval methods, memory systems, safety classifiers, and interfaces will all change over years.</p>
      <div class="dc-pill-row"><span class="dc-pill">model A</span><span class="dc-pill">model B</span><span class="dc-pill">local model</span><span class="dc-pill">school-approved model</span></div>
    </div>
    <div>
      <h2>The Ark must outlast the engines.</h2>
      <p class="dc-bigline">The Steward can use better engines without making the student start again.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
</div>

---

<div class="dc-shell">
  <div class="dc-kicker">The Conscience</div>
  <div class="dc-grid-2">
    <div>
      <h2>The boundary with judgement.</h2>
      <p class="dc-bigline">The Conscience governs what crosses the Ark boundary and explains why.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="dc-card">
      <h3>It checks</h3>
      <ul>
        <li>school rules, family permissions, law, safety, privacy and cost;</li>
        <li>what the Steward may do;</li>
        <li>what parents or teachers may see;</li>
        <li>when escalation is required.</li>
      </ul>
    </div>
  </div>
</div>

---

<div class="dc-shell">
  <div class="dc-kicker">The Covenant</div>
  <div class="dc-grid-2">
    <div class="dc-card">
      <h3>Not the container</h3>
      <p>The Covenant is not the Ark. It is the promise around the Ark: who may steer, see, certify, recover, contribute, or intervene.</p>
      <h3 style="margin-top: 22px;">School phrase</h3>
      <p>“The Covenant defines how school, family, student, and system share responsibility.”</p>
    </div>
    <div>
      <h2>The Ark is owned. The Covenant is agreed.</h2>
      <p class="dc-bigline">This keeps care from becoming surveillance.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
</div>

---

<div class="dc-shell">
  <div class="dc-kicker">Vault and Codex</div>
  <div class="dc-grid-2">
    <div class="dc-card"><h3>Vault</h3><p>The hardened inner chamber: credentials, keys, money rails, biometric recovery, export permissions and other high-risk controls.</p><p class="dc-muted">Phrase: “That belongs in the Vault.”</p></div>
    <div class="dc-card"><h3>Codex</h3><p>The living body of knowledge: learning evidence, reflections, projects, feedback, mistakes, values, preferences, and school-certified records.</p><p class="dc-muted">Phrase: “The Codex is how knowledge compounds.”</p></div>
  </div>
</div>

---

<div class="dc-shell">
  <div class="dc-kicker">Ledger and Ports</div>
  <div class="dc-grid-2">
    <div class="dc-card"><h3>Ledger</h3><p>Provenance and accountability: what happened, why it happened, whose influence shaped it, what model/tool was used, and how to audit or reverse it.</p></div>
    <div class="dc-card"><h3>Ports</h3><p>Export, import, restore, migrate, connect, and leave. Ports prove the Ark is portable and not a corporate prison.</p></div>
  </div>
  <p class="dc-bigline" style="margin-top: 28px; max-width: 1000px;">Ownership is not a slogan until the student can move the Ark.</p>
</div>

---

<div class="dc-shell">
  <div class="dc-kicker">Student ownership</div>
  <div class="dc-grid-2">
    <div>
      <h2>Portable by design.</h2>
      <p class="dc-bigline">The Ark must be open enough to leave with, restore elsewhere, and survive the school/vendor/tool stack changing.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="dc-card">
      <h3>Proof points</h3>
      <ul>
        <li>export and restore drills;</li>
        <li>open source or open protocol where possible;</li>
        <li>replaceable engines and tools;</li>
        <li>clear custody and recovery rights;</li>
        <li>deletion and revocation that actually work.</li>
      </ul>
    </div>
  </div>
</div>

---

<div class="dc-shell">
  <div class="dc-kicker">How people should talk</div>
  <h2>Ordinary language matters.</h2>
  <div class="dc-grid-3" style="margin-top: 24px;">
    <div class="dc-card"><h3>School</h3><p>“We are piloting student Arks, tuning the Conscience, and agreeing the Covenant with families.”</p></div>
    <div class="dc-card"><h3>Parent</h3><p>“I can contribute to my child’s Ark without taking it over.”</p></div>
    <div class="dc-card"><h3>Student</h3><p>“This is my Ark. My Steward helps me. The Conscience tells me why something is blocked.”</p></div>
  </div>
</div>

---

<div class="dc-shell">
  <div class="dc-kicker">How it fits existing decks</div>
  <div class="dc-grid-2">
    <div class="dc-card">
      <h3>Old language</h3>
      <ul><li>enduring core</li><li>durable core</li><li>child core</li><li>parent core</li><li>container / capsule</li></ul>
    </div>
    <div class="dc-card">
      <h3>New language</h3>
      <ul><li>student Ark</li><li>parent Ark</li><li>school Ark</li><li>simulated-student Ark</li><li>portable Ark</li></ul>
    </div>
  </div>
  <p class="dc-muted" style="font-size: .46em; margin-top: 24px;">Keep “core/container” only when explaining the underlying architecture. Public language should lead with Ark.</p>
</div>

---

<div class="dc-shell">
  <div class="dc-kicker">Reference sentence</div>
  <h2>Every child needs an Ark, not another app.</h2>
  <p class="dc-bigline" style="max-width: 1120px;">A durable personal Ark where knowledge, tools, memory, values and agency can accumulate; a Steward works inside it, a Conscience guards its boundary, and a Covenant defines how family and school share responsibility.</p>
</div>
