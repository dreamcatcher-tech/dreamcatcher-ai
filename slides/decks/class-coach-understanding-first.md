<div class="dc-shell">
  <div class="dc-kicker">The key shift</div>
  <div class="dc-grid-2">
    <div>
      <h2>The product is not the exam.</h2>
      <p class="dc-bigline">The product is proving understanding well enough that the exam becomes the benchmark.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="dc-card">
      <h3>What changes</h3>
      <ul>
        <li>Class Coach teaches and probes for understanding before exam technique.</li>
        <li>Exam-style tasks are used periodically to calibrate readiness.</li>
        <li>The student is not judged by one day alone; evidence accumulates across time.</li>
        <li>Parents see pace, risk and next moves while the learning is still fixable.</li>
      </ul>
    </div>
  </div>
  <p class="dc-quote" style="margin-top: 30px;">If understanding is real, durable and transferable, exam performance should become the consequence.</p>
</div>

---

<div class="dc-shell">
  <div class="dc-kicker">What counts as proof</div>
  <h2>Understanding has to show up in more than one format.</h2>
  <div class="dc-grid-3" style="margin-top: 28px;">
    <article class="dc-card"><h3>Explain it</h3><p>The student can say why the concept works in their own words.</p></article>
    <article class="dc-card"><h3>Transfer it</h3><p>They can apply the idea to a new example, not just the rehearsed one.</p></article>
    <article class="dc-card"><h3>Build it</h3><p>A tiny app, model, diagram or generated image makes the concept manipulable.</p></article>
  </div>
  <div class="dc-grid-3" style="margin-top: 18px;">
    <article class="dc-card"><h3>Challenge it</h3><p>They can spot misconceptions, traps and false patterns.</p></article>
    <article class="dc-card"><h3>Teach it back</h3><p>They can help the AI or another learner see the idea clearly.</p></article>
    <article class="dc-card"><h3>Retrieve it later</h3><p>They can bring it back after time has passed, without being led.</p></article>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="dc-shell">
  <div class="dc-kicker">Teacher reports and evidence integrity</div>
  <div class="dc-grid-2">
    <div>
      <h2>Teachers should not need an exam setting to know where the class is.</h2>
      <p class="dc-bigline" style="font-size: 0.88em; line-height: 1.10;">Progress, homework, stale topics and misconceptions — reported as permitted learning evidence.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="dc-card">
      <h3>Integrity checks for higher-stakes evidence</h3>
      <ul>
        <li>Short oral checks with consented speaker verification.</li>
        <li>Response timing in voice or text as a confidence signal.</li>
        <li>Consistency with the learner’s wider understanding trail.</li>
        <li>Human review when a signal affects a badge or intervention.</li>
      </ul>
      <p class="dc-muted" style="font-size: 0.42em; line-height: 1.25; margin-top: 16px;">Frame this as evidence integrity, not surveillance: proportionate, consented, policy-governed and never based on one signal alone.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
</div>

---

<div class="dc-shell">
  <div class="dc-kicker">Memory management</div>
  <div class="dc-grid-2">
    <div>
      <h2>Retention is part of the product.</h2>
      <p class="dc-bigline">Class Coach should revisit topics before they fade — and teach the student how to remember better.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="dc-card">
      <h3>Memory loop</h3>
      <ul>
        <li>Schedule spaced retrieval for concepts at risk of decay.</li>
        <li>Interleave old and new topics so knowledge stays flexible.</li>
        <li>Teach active recall, chunking, visual hooks and memory palace methods where useful.</li>
        <li>Use delayed checks as evidence of durable understanding.</li>
      </ul>
    </div>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="dc-shell">
  <div class="dc-kicker">Expert modules</div>
  <h2>Some modules can be AI-native books.</h2>
  <div class="dc-grid-3" style="margin-top: 30px;">
    <article class="dc-card"><h3>Author method</h3><p>An expert provides the method, examples, constraints, rubric and practice pathway.</p></article>
    <article class="dc-card"><h3>Interactive coach</h3><p>The AI teaches the method on the student's real school content and adapts the practice.</p></article>
    <article class="dc-card"><h3>Feedback channel</h3><p>With permission, subscription feedback can help the author or expert team improve the module.</p></article>
  </div>
  <p class="dc-quote" style="margin-top: 30px;">Instead of buying only a book, the student gets a method that watches them practise.</p>
</div>

---

<div class="dc-shell">
  <div class="dc-kicker">Badges</div>
  <div class="dc-grid-2">
    <div class="dc-card">
      <h3>A badge should say what was proven</h3>
      <ul>
        <li>concept or capability;</li>
        <li>evidence types completed;</li>
        <li>transfer and misconception checks;</li>
        <li>delay / retention period;</li>
        <li>module provenance and renewal rule.</li>
      </ul>
    </div>
    <div>
      <h2>Not “I completed the lesson.”</h2>
      <p class="dc-bigline">“I demonstrated understanding under these conditions, and I retained it over time.”</p>
      <p class="dc-muted" style="font-size: 0.52em; line-height: 1.28;">Badges support parents, teachers and students. They do not replace official qualifications or teacher judgement.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
</div>

---

<div class="dc-shell">
  <div class="dc-kicker">Long-term evidence</div>
  <h2>Assessment can happen over time, not only on one day.</h2>
  <div class="dc-grid-3" style="margin-top: 30px;">
    <article class="dc-card"><h3>Week 1</h3><p>The student can explain the concept immediately after learning.</p></article>
    <article class="dc-card"><h3>Week 3</h3><p>The student can retrieve it after delay and apply it in a fresh setting.</p></article>
    <article class="dc-card"><h3>Week 6</h3><p>An exam-style calibration checks whether the evidence predicts readiness.</p></article>
  </div>
  <p class="dc-quote" style="margin-top: 32px;">The best proof is not a single performance. It is understanding that survives time, context changes and pressure.</p>
</div>

  <div class="dc-grid-2">

      <p class="dc-bigline">Then use the exam as the benchmark.</p>
