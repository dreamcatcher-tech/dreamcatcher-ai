---

<div class="dc-shell">
  <div class="dc-kicker">Why this separate deck exists</div>
  <div class="dc-grid-2">
    <div>
      <h2>There is a resource strategy hiding inside the safety problem.</h2>
      <p class="dc-bigline">Make child safety the first thing to get right — and the first thing to measure publicly.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="dc-card">
      <h3>The parent-facing message</h3>
      <p>We are not asking for child-facing Arks before the boundary is proven. We are asking the school to validate the boundary that would make release responsible.</p>
      <h3 style="margin-top: 22px;">The external-facing message</h3>
      <p>This is a child/youth safety, parent-control, and education deployment research problem — the kind where outside partners may want to contribute.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
</div>

---

<div class="dc-shell">
  <div class="dc-kicker">The main wedge</div>
  <div class="dc-grid-2">
    <div class="dc-card">
      <h3>Lead with the thing everyone can get behind</h3>
      <p>Child safety is not a side constraint. It is the shared first priority: parents, school leadership, teachers, students, external AI partners, and regulators can all understand it.</p>
      <h3 style="margin-top: 22px;">What that does strategically</h3>
      <p>It turns caution into momentum: here is the limit, here is the blocker, here is how we are testing it, and here is what can happen safely meanwhile.</p>
    </div>
    <div>
      <h2>Don’t bury the blocker.</h2>
      <p class="dc-bigline">Hold it up as the first research object.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
</div>

---

<div class="dc-shell">
  <div class="dc-kicker">Release gate</div>
  <h2>What must be true before child-facing release?</h2>
  <div class="dc-grid-3" style="margin-top: 28px;">
    <div class="dc-card"><h3>Conscience</h3><p>Can the boundary block, redirect, explain, and record decisions across inbound/outbound actions?</p></div>
    <div class="dc-card"><h3>Custody and privacy</h3><p>Can the Ark protect child data, avoid unnecessary raw visibility, support export/delete/restore, and obey age/data rules?</p></div>
    <div class="dc-card"><h3>Escalation</h3><p>Can wellbeing or safety concerns move through soft/orange/red human-overseen pathways without becoming covert surveillance?</p></div>
  </div>
  <p class="dc-muted" style="font-size: .48em; margin-top: 28px;">This is a release gate, not a slogan. If the gate fails, student rollout waits while adult and simulated sandboxes continue.</p>
</div>

<div class="dc-shell">
  <div class="dc-kicker">OpenAI-facing avenue</div>
  <div class="dc-grid-2">
    <div>
      <h2>Frame the ask around youth safety and evidence.</h2>
      <p class="dc-bigline">Credits and engineering help are plausible asks only if the pilot produces reusable safety learning.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="dc-card">
      <h3>Grounding from OpenAI sources</h3>
      <ul>
        <li>OpenAI publicly emphasizes safe, age-appropriate AI for young people and parental/guardian controls.</li>
        <li>OpenAI’s education rollout language stresses phased deployments, local leaders, teacher training, and research.</li>
        <li>OpenAI’s grant portal says grants may be monetary compensation or API credits depending on the program.</li>
      </ul>
      <p class="dc-muted" style="font-size: .40em; margin-top: 16px;">Important: this does not promise support. It defines the strongest route to ask.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
</div>

---

<div class="dc-shell">
  <div class="dc-kicker">What we would ask for</div>
  <h2>Make the ask specific enough to be useful.</h2>
  <div class="dc-grid-3" style="margin-top: 28px;">
    <div class="dc-card"><h3>1 · Credits</h3><p>API/model credits for simulated-child scenarios, Conscience benchmarks, dashboard generation, and parent/staff sandbox workloads.</p></div>
    <div class="dc-card"><h3>2 · Engineering review</h3><p>Advice on age-appropriate behaviour, safety evaluations, red-team harnesses, privacy boundaries, and escalation design.</p></div>
    <div class="dc-card"><h3>3 · Legitimacy / visibility</h3><p>Permissioned co-design language, partner credibility, and a safer path to invite other schools into the measurement board later.</p></div>
  </div>
  <p class="dc-muted" style="font-size: .46em; margin-top: 28px;">The ask should be tied to deliverables: benchmark set, safety report, privacy/custody model, and monthly evidence updates.</p>
</div>

---

<div class="dc-shell">
  <div class="dc-kicker">Support-needs pilot wedge</div>
  <div class="dc-grid-2">
    <div class="dc-card">
      <h3>One concrete avenue: navigation support</h3>
      <p>A consented assistant that helps a family navigate learning-support pathways: documents, service options, meeting prep, timelines, questions to ask, and next actions.</p>
      <h3 style="margin-top: 22px;">Why it fits</h3>
      <p>It is child-safety-adjacent, parent-legible, service-oriented, and easier to justify as research than a general “student chatbot.”</p>
    </div>
    <div>
      <h2>Not diagnosis. Not entitlement decisions.</h2>
      <p class="dc-bigline">A navigator through fragmented help schemes.</p>
      <p class="dc-muted" style="font-size: .44em; line-height: 1.28; margin-top: 18px;">NZ language first: learning support / additional needs. Mention SEND only when referring to UK-style external examples.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
</div>

---

<div class="dc-shell">
  <div class="dc-kicker">What other schools and systems are doing</div>
  <h2>Use external examples as reference cases, not copy-paste templates.</h2>
  <div class="dc-grid-3" style="margin-top: 28px;">
    <div class="dc-card"><h3>Estonia + OpenAI</h3><p>National deployment reference: phased education rollout with researchers studying impact.</p></div>
    <div class="dc-card"><h3>SchoolAI</h3><p>OpenAI-published case: teacher oversight, progress signals, personalized support, large source-reported scale.</p></div>
    <div class="dc-card"><h3>Arco Educação</h3><p>OpenAI-published case: teacher assistant for lesson planning and inclusion support, with privacy controls.</p></div>
  </div>
  <p class="dc-muted" style="font-size: .46em; margin-top: 26px;">NZ baseline: Ministry guidance says schools should make policy, avoid personal data in unsafe tools, preserve teacher responsibility, and handle NCEA authenticity.</p>
</div>

<div class="dc-shell">
  <div class="dc-kicker">Proof-point board rules</div>
  <div class="dc-grid-2">
    <div>
      <h2>The board should not rank hype.</h2>
      <p class="dc-bigline">It should track evidence that a school is learning safely.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="dc-card">
      <h3>Publishable metrics</h3>
      <ul>
        <li>Policy and consent completeness.</li>
        <li>Number and type of sandbox scenarios tested.</li>
        <li>Conscience benchmark pass/fail and explanation quality.</li>
        <li>Parent and teacher participation.</li>
        <li>Incidents, reversals, and lessons learned.</li>
        <li>Cost per Ark, token burn, escalation load, and support load.</li>
      </ul>
    </div>
  </div>
</div>

---

<div class="dc-shell">
  <div class="dc-kicker">What can happen while the blocker remains closed</div>
  <h2>Sandboxing keeps momentum without pretending the release gate is solved.</h2>
  <div class="dc-grid-3" style="margin-top: 28px;">
    <div class="dc-card"><h3>Adult Arks</h3><p>Staff and parent partners use real Arks for adult work, reflection, and feedback.</p></div>
    <div class="dc-card"><h3>Simulated children</h3><p>Synthetic child Arks exercise hard cases: secrecy, unsafe asks, overuse, bullying, confusion, money, escalation.</p></div>
    <div class="dc-card"><h3>Problem register</h3><p>Every concern, request, idea, and cost trigger enters a visible pipeline instead of evaporating into meetings.</p></div>
  </div>
</div>

---

<div class="dc-shell">
  <div class="dc-kicker">Parent-facing page</div>
  <div class="dc-grid-2">
    <div class="dc-card">
      <h3>What parents need to see</h3>
      <ul>
        <li>What the school is pushing toward.</li>
        <li>What current limitation blocks release.</li>
        <li>What sandbox experiments are safe now.</li>
        <li>What would count as evidence of readiness.</li>
        <li>How parents can help without pushing children into the blast radius.</li>
      </ul>
    </div>
    <div>
      <h2>Make caution visible.</h2>
      <p class="dc-bigline">A clear blocker is easier to support than vague delay.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
</div>

---

<div class="dc-shell">
  <div class="dc-kicker">Newsletter / field notes</div>
  <h2>Keep the board alive with a monthly update.</h2>
  <div class="dc-grid-3" style="margin-top: 28px;">
    <div class="dc-card"><h3>1 · Safety gate</h3><p>What passed, what failed, what was changed, what remains blocked.</p></div>
    <div class="dc-card"><h3>2 · Resource radar</h3><p>OpenAI / foundation / university / school-partner avenues being pursued, with status and next ask.</p></div>
    <div class="dc-card"><h3>3 · Benchmark board</h3><p>What other schools and systems are doing, and what evidence the school can compare against.</p></div>
  </div>
  <p class="dc-muted" style="font-size: .46em; margin-top: 26px;">Suggested title: <strong>School AI Safety Field Notes</strong> — short, source-linked, parent-readable.</p>
</div>

---

<div class="dc-shell">
  <div class="dc-kicker">90-day path</div>
  <div class="dc-grid-3" style="margin-top: 28px;">
    <div class="dc-card"><h3>First 30 days</h3><p>Define child-safety gate, draft parent page, choose sandbox cohorts, create measurement board, prepare OpenAI ask packet.</p></div>
    <div class="dc-card"><h3>Days 31–60</h3><p>Run staff/parent adult Ark sandboxes, simulated-child scenarios, Conscience benchmark v1, and support-needs navigator prototype.</p></div>
    <div class="dc-card"><h3>Days 61–90</h3><p>Publish first field notes, compare against reference cases, revise blocker list, decide whether any narrow real-student micro-pilot is ethically ready.</p></div>
  </div>
</div>

---

<div class="dc-shell">
  <div class="dc-kicker">One-page ask packet</div>
  <div class="dc-grid-2">
    <div>
      <h2>If we approach OpenAI, make it measurable.</h2>
      <p class="dc-bigline">“Help us research child-safe Arks in a school setting.”</p>
    </div>
    <div class="dc-card">
      <h3>Packet contents</h3>
      <ul>
        <li>Child-safety gate and release blocker.</li>
        <li>Sandbox-only plan and consent model.</li>
        <li>Conscience benchmark and simulated-child scenario library.</li>
        <li>Learning-support navigator wedge, with SEND only as an external reference.</li>
        <li>Measurement board and newsletter cadence.</li>
        <li>Specific ask: credits, technical review, safety/evaluation advice, and permitted visibility.</li>
      </ul>
    </div>
  </div>
</div>
