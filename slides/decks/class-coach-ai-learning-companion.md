<div class="dc-shell">
  <div class="dc-kicker">Product definition</div>
  <h2>One companion from the start of the learning journey.</h2>
  <div class="dc-grid-3" style="margin-top: 32px;">
    <article class="dc-card"><h3>Every lesson</h3><p>Class Coach sits with the student through each lesson in the permitted classroom mode.</p></article>
    <article class="dc-card"><h3>Every gap</h3><p>When a concept does not land, the companion keeps working with the child during class, break, homework, or later revision.</p></article>
    <article class="dc-card"><h3>Every learner</h3><p>It remembers what explanations, examples, languages, and practice patterns have helped this student before.</p></article>
  </div>
  <div class="dc-card" style="margin-top: 28px; border-color: rgba(88,215,211,.34); background: rgba(88,215,211,.08);">
    <h3>Positioning</h3>
    <p>Not a generic chatbot. A school-contextual learning companion designed around the student’s actual lessons and understanding.</p>
  </div>
</div>

---

<div class="dc-shell">
  <div class="dc-kicker">What it understands</div>
  <div class="dc-grid-2">
    <div>
      <h2>Class Coach works from the lesson the child is actually in.</h2>
      <p class="dc-bigline">Curriculum, lesson plan, learner profile, and teacher intent come together.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="dc-card">
      <h3>Approved context</h3>
      <ul>
        <li>International Baccalaureate (IB) curriculum and unit objectives where applicable.</li>
        <li>Teacher lesson plans, vocabulary and resources.</li>
        <li>Prerequisites and common misconceptions.</li>
        <li>The student’s preferred learning methods and confidence patterns.</li>
      </ul>
    </div>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="dc-shell">
  <div class="dc-kicker">Personal learning profile</div>
  <h2>It learns how this child learns.</h2>
  <div class="dc-grid-3" style="margin-top: 32px;">
    <article class="dc-card"><h3>Language</h3><p>Explains in the child’s terms: simpler, more precise, visual, metaphorical, bilingual, or step-by-step.</p></article>
    <article class="dc-card"><h3>Method</h3><p>Finds the route that works: analogy, worked example, Socratic prompt, diagram, memory hook, or quick practice.</p></article>
    <article class="dc-card"><h3>Pace</h3><p>Repeats patiently without social pressure, then checks for transfer rather than moving on too quickly.</p></article>
  </div>
  <p class="dc-quote" style="margin-top: 30px;">A highly qualified, patient and kind tutor — with the humility to explain it another way.</p>
</div>

---

<div class="dc-shell">
  <div class="dc-kicker">Teacher loop</div>
  <div class="dc-grid-2">
    <div class="dc-card">
      <h3>What teachers should receive</h3>
      <ul>
        <li>Concepts the student is still struggling with.</li>
        <li>Evidence that understanding has improved.</li>
        <li>Confidence and engagement signals from Class Coach work.</li>
        <li>Suggested small-group or individual support moves.</li>
      </ul>
    </div>
    <div>
      <h2>Useful feedback, not surveillance.</h2>
      <p class="dc-bigline">Private enough to be honest. Visible enough for teachers to help.</p>
      <p class="dc-muted">The teacher loop should use permitted summaries and learning evidence — not raw private conversations by default.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
</div>

---

<div class="dc-shell">
  <div class="dc-kicker">How it evolves</div>
  <h2>From tutor to learning-support companion.</h2>
  <div class="dc-grid-3" style="margin-top: 30px;">
    <article class="dc-card"><h3>1 · Onboarding</h3><p>Learns the student’s subjects, language, confidence pattern and helpful explanation styles.</p></article>
    <article class="dc-card"><h3>2 · Daily support</h3><p>Unblocks class concepts, homework, revision and study planning with continuity.</p></article>
    <article class="dc-card"><h3>3 · Projects</h3><p>Coaches research plans, source discipline, drafts, presentations and reflection.</p></article>
  </div>
  <div class="dc-grid-2" style="margin-top: 22px;">
    <article class="dc-card"><h3>4 · Evidence portfolio</h3><p>Builds permitted records of learning growth, strengths, misconceptions and achievements.</p></article>
    <article class="dc-card"><h3>5 · Transition support</h3><p>Helps students carry useful learning context across terms, year levels and future pathways.</p></article>
  </div>
</div>
