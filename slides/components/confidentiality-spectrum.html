Open design question

The visibility boundary must be tuned, not guessed

The pilot should make the trade-offs visible: child agency, parent trust, school duty, safety, and attribution.

Full confidentiality

No one can see inside the child’s core. Strong interior privacy, but parents and school may lack enough visibility to help, trust, or discharge duties.

Full visibility

Everyone sees everything. Easy oversight, but it collapses privacy, agency, trust, and the child’s protected interior life.

The answer is almost certainly in between.Experiments tune what is private, what is summarized, what is attributed, what is escalated, and what requires explicit approval.
