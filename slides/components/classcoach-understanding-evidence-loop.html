Evidence loop
Class Coach keeps asking: what proves the understanding has landed?

Teach

Explain through the student's lesson context, language, examples and modalities.

Demonstrate

Ask for evidence: explain, transfer, build, visualize, correct, retrieve.

Model

Update confidence, misconceptions, prerequisites and pace against the plan.

Revisit

Schedule retrieval before forgetting, then check whether understanding persists.

Calibrate

Use exam-style tasks periodically to compare the model with the external benchmark.

Badge

Record durable understanding with evidence types, delay, provenance and limits.
