Learning loop
From missed concept to teacher-actionable evidence.
1

Approved lesson context

Curriculum, unit goals, lesson plan, vocabulary and teacher intent.

2

Private question

The child asks in their own words without performing confusion in public.

3

Personal explanation

Class Coach tries the method this child has found helpful before.

4

Understanding check

Practice, retrieval, transfer and confidence checks test whether it landed.

5

Permitted teacher signal

Concept gaps and useful evidence flow back without exposing raw private chats.
